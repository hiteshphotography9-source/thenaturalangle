THE NATURAL ANGLE

Hitesh Chawla

Wildlife photographer · Educator · Traveller

“Technique gets you a correctly exposed, sharp frame. Fieldcraft gets you a frame worth taking.”

Hitesh picked up a DSLR in 2012 and never put it down. Fourteen years and more than fifty Indian locations later, he leads photography tours across nine reserves, writes books, and teaches what the field taught him.

He believes photography is a universal language, one that rewards patience over impatience and observation over assumption.
