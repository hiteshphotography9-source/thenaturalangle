FAQ

I’m a beginner. Is this for me?

Yes. You’ll get your settings set up before the first drive and help in the jeep when it matters.

What if we don’t see a tiger?

No sighting is ever guaranteed. These are wild animals on their own schedule. Every drive is spent reading the forest, the alarm calls and the light, so the frames are about more than the big cat.

Can a non-photographer come along?

Yes. The photography guidance is part of the trip, but you are welcome to simply watch and enjoy the safaris. Message Hitesh on WhatsApp if you want to confirm for your group.
