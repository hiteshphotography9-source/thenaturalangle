Questions

Everything owners ask before booking.

How much does a hotel shoot cost?

Less than most owners expect, and far less than one month of rooms sold at a discount. Packages are built for every size of property, from a 15-room hotel in Paharganj to a full resort. Send your number of room types on WhatsApp and you get an exact, fixed quote within 2-3 hours. No hidden charges.

How fast will I get my photos?

Final, hand-edited photos are delivered within 3 days of the shoot, in a shared folder you can download from anywhere. Need them sooner for a launch or an OTA deadline? Mention it when you enquire.

Will the shoot disturb my guests or staff?

No. The shoot is planned room by room around your occupancy. Vacant rooms first, public areas at quiet hours. Check-ins, housekeeping and service carry on as normal.

How long does the shoot take on the day?

Most hotels are covered in a few hours on a single day. Larger properties and resorts are planned in advance so nothing is rushed.

Do I need to prepare the rooms?

Just keep them clean and made up. I style every frame myself: straightening linen, placing cushions, adjusting curtains and lights, and moving clutter out of shot. You will get a short checklist before the shoot.

Will the photos work on MakeMyTrip, Booking.com and Google?

Yes. Every photo is delivered in the right sizes for OTAs, Google Business Profile, Airbnb, your website and print. They are ready to upload the same day you receive them.

Do you over-edit or fake the rooms?

Never. Guests who feel misled leave bad reviews. I correct light, colour and lines so the room looks exactly as good as it is in real life, at its best moment.

What is the free photo audit?

Send your listing link on WhatsApp. I review your current photos and tell you honestly what is costing you clicks and what to fix first, with a fixed quote. No obligation to book.

Can you shoot restaurants, offices and homes too?

Yes. Alongside 40+ hotels I have shot restaurants, corporate offices and residential projects, including work for Rajan Wadhwa Associates.

Do you travel outside Delhi?

Yes, across India. Recent shoots include Shimla and Bodhgaya, along with V Resorts properties in Uttarakhand and Maharashtra. Travel is planned into your quote upfront.

Can I use the photos for ads and print?

Yes. The photos are yours to use on your listings, website, social media, ads, brochures and signage.
