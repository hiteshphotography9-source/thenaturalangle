## Indian Pitta "Navrang" reel (34 s, about 2.5 words per second)
1. Hook 0.2 to 3.0: Is chhoti si chidiya ke fluff ke neeche, kya chhupa hai?
2. Front 3.2 to 8.8: Saamne se dekho. Bhoori, seedhi si lagti hai. Ab rang ginte jao. Ek... do... teen... chaar...
3. Back 9.2 to 14.8: Ab ise ghumao. Peeth par zamurrad jaisa hara. Aur dum ke upar... electric neela!
4. Hunter 15.2 to 20.8: Par ye sirf sundar nahi, shikaari hai. Zameen par patte palat-ti hai. Cheenti, deemak, ghonghe. Aur... pakad liya.
5. Map 21.3 to 26.9: Ye baarish ki chidiya hai. Monsoon mein ghonsla banati hai. Sardiyon mein, dakshin ki taraf.
6. Finale 27.8 to 33.6: Naam hai Navrang. Matlab, nau rang. Ek chhoti si chidiya. Aage ya peeche, aapko kaunsa side zyada pasand aaya? Comment mein batao.

## Laggar falcon reel (used, VO recorded and cleaned)
1. Falcon matlab chidiya ka shikaari, hai na? Par kya sach mein sirf chidiya?
2. 2019 mein ek Laggar falcon ke ghonsle ko dekha gaya. Bachchon ko khilaya gaya, seventy nine percent lizard.
3. Usi ghonsle mein sixteen tarah ka shikaar. Reptiles, mammals, aur birds. Menu bahut bada.
4. Ye hamesha oonchi udaan nahi bharta. Kisi perch se, ya zameen ke bilkul paas, tez aur neeche.
5. Ghonsla khud nahi banata. Kauwon aur giddhon ke purane ghonsle le leta hai, ek tinka bhi nahi jodta.
6. Aam taur par ek hazaar meter se neeche rehta hai. Par ek baar Ladakh mein, chaar hazaar teen sau saath meter par dikha.
7. Safar aasaan nahi. Das saal mein iski aabaadi shayad bees se untees percent gir gayi. Wajah: pesticide, habitat ki kami, aur bait ke liye pakda jaana.
8. Agli baar kisi khambe par falcon dikhe, to ek baar dobara dekhna. Wo Laggar ho sakta hai. Aapne kahan dekha? Comment mein batao.
