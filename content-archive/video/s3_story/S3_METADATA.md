**Description**
S3, also called Surai 3 and Manag, is one of the biggest male tigers of Pilibhit Tiger Reserve, Uttar Pradesh. Local safari sources report a weight of about 280 to 300 kg and a length of about 10 feet, and say his biggest rival is S2, who once nearly killed him before forest officials rescued him.

Fact vs reported: the weight, length, rival and rescue are as told by local safari sources. I found no official measurement or public report, so treat them as reported, not confirmed. For comparison, measured wild Bengal males are usually 200 to 260 kg, and the heaviest free-ranging males reported (Chitwan) were over 270 kg.

Pilibhit Tiger Reserve: about 730 sq km, a tiger reserve since June 2014, part of the Terai Arc and linked to Dudhwa. Maps are illustrative.
