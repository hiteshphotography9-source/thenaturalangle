**Description**
S3, also known as Surai 3 and Monarch, is a male tiger of Pilibhit Tiger Reserve, Uttar Pradesh, about 6 to 7 years old. Local guides report a weight of about 280 to 300 kg and a length of about 10 feet (not officially measured). His biggest rival is S2, who once attacked him so badly he nearly died, until forest officials rescued him.

For comparison, measured wild Bengal males are usually 200 to 260 kg, and the heaviest free-ranging males reported (Chitwan) were over 270 kg.
