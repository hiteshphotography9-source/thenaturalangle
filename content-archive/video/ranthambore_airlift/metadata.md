## YOUTUBE SHORT DESCRIPTION
In 2005 a report said Sariska had no tigers left. In 2008, tigers from Ranthambore were airlifted in. The first male died in 2010 (reported poisoning). The tigress, ST-2, survived. Reports say most of Sariska's tigers are her family, and in 2025 Sariska unveiled India's first tiger memorial for her.
