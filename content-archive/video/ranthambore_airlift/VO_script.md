# The Airlift: VO script (Hinglish, about 39 s)

One calm take, close to the mic. Leave about 0.4 s of silence between lines. I will tighten the pauses. The gut-punch section (0:14 to 0:19) is SILENT on purpose, with only a heartbeat monitor sound. Do not talk over it, except line 5 which starts at 0:15.

| Time | On screen | Say this |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 to 0:03 | Cub's eyes, big words | Ek tiger ko udaakar, ek khaali jungle mein le jaaya gaya. |
| 0:03 to 0:06 | Map zooms into Sariska, big "0" | Sariska, 2005. Report aayi: ek bhi tiger nahi bacha. |
| 0:06 to 0:09 | Ranthambore photo | Sirf 150 kilometre door, Ranthambore ke tigers theek the. |
| 0:09 to 0:14 | Helicopter on the map | 2008 mein, Ranthambore se tigers ko hawai raaste se Sariska bheja gaya. |

| 0:19 to 0:24 | Tigress comes out of the dark | Lekin ek tigress zinda rahi. ST-2. Ranthambore se aayi thi. |
| 0:24 to 0:29 | Family tree grows, 30+ | Reports ke mutabik, aaj Sariska ke zyadatar tigers usi ke parivaar se hain. Tees se zyada. |

| 0:34 to 0:39 | Cub close-up, question | Kya tigers ko aise shift karna sahi hai? Haan ya na, comment karo. |

## Delivery
- Line 1 is the hook. Low, serious, almost a whisper. Slow on "khaali jungle".
- Line 5: say it flat and quiet. This is the emotional low point. Pause a full second after "mara gaya".
- Line 6: breath in, then lift your energy slightly. This is the turn.
- Last line: direct, curious, like a real question to a friend.
- Keep these facts as written: 2005, 150 km, 2008, ST-1, 2010, "reports ke mutabik", ST-2, 30+, 2025.

## Two alternate hooks (test the first 3 s)
1. Sariska mein ek bhi tiger nahi bacha tha. Phir Ranthambore ne ek tiger udaakar bheja.
2. Ek tiger ko helicopter se ek jungle mein chhoda gaya. Do saal baad, wo mara gaya.
