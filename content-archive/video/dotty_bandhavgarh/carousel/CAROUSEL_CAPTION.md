# Dotty carousel (8 slides, 1080x1440)

## Instagram caption
Comment DOTTY if you ever saw her, and tell me where.

She was born with a "D" on her forehead. That is how Bandhavgarh knew her.

Born about 2012 in Magadhi. Daughter of Sukhi Patiha, sister of Spotty. She raised her litters in Magadhi until her own grown daughters pushed her out. Most tigresses fade after that. Dotty moved to Tala and became a mother again.

On 9 October 2026 she was found in the Manpur buffer range during an elephant patrol. About 14 years old. Preliminary examination: natural causes.

She leaves two cubs, 15 to 16 months old. Cubs usually stay with their mother for 18 to 24 months, so these two are months short of independence. The forest department is watching over them.

Her son D1 is reported to be the King of Khitauli. Her line still walks Bandhavgarh.

Save this for the next time you are in Tala or Magadhi.
