**Description**
Dotty, one of Bandhavgarh Tiger Reserve's most famous tigresses, was found dead on 9 October 2026 in the Majhkheta beat of the Manpur buffer range, during an elephant patrol. She was about 14. The Field Director said a preliminary examination indicates natural causes. She leaves two cubs, 15 to 16 months old, now being monitored by the forest department.

Named for the "D" mark on her forehead. Born about 2012 in Magadhi zone to Sukhi Patiha, sister of Spotty. Pushed out of her territory by her own daughters, she moved to Tala and became a mother again. D1, the dominant male of Khitauli, is reported to be her son.
