# Dotty of Bandhavgarh: research file and VO script (draft, 12 Oct 2026)

## Facts by confidence
HIGH (several news outlets, Oct 2026)
- Dotty, a famous tigress of Bandhavgarh Tiger Reserve, Madhya Pradesh. Named for a "D"-shaped mark on the right side of her forehead.
- Found dead on Friday 9 Oct 2026, Majhkheta beat, Manpur buffer range, during an elephant patrol.
- Field Director Dr Anupam Sahay: preliminary examination indicates natural causes. Area secured per NTCA SOP. Post-mortem on Saturday 10 Oct, then cremation. Samples sent for lab tests.
- Age reported about 14 (one outlet says 15).
- She leaves two cubs aged 15 to 16 months; the reserve is monitoring them.
MEDIUM (multiple guide/safari sites, not official)
- Born about 2012 in Magadhi zone. Mother: Sukhi (Sookhi) Patiha. Sister: Spotty (T-41).
- Raised litters in Magadhi (seen with cubs in 2015 and 2019 reports).
- Her own daughters pushed her out of her territory; she moved to Tala zone and had two more cubs there (Hans India summary).
LOW (single source or conflicting; do not state as fact)
- Father: "Blue Eyes" vs New Charger (T-6). Sources conflict.
- Mates: Mahaman, or Bamera's son "Mr X". Sources conflict.
- D1, the dominant male of Khitauli, is described as Dotty's son; reported to have killed male Pujari in a fight in May 2026.
- One local outlet (Mediawala) called the death suspicious and claimed a recent attack on a villager. Not confirmed by any other source.
- One site says a "Dotty" died in June 2018: likely a different tigress or an error.

## Sources
Hans India, NewsBytes, Univarta, Amar Ujala, ETV Bharat, People's Update (death, Oct 2026); Mediawala (suspicious-death claim); bandhavgarh.net, bandhavgarhnationalpark.net, pugdundeesafaris.com, tigerwalah.com, thewildlifeindia.com (lineage, D1, Pujari).

## VO script (Hinglish, about 55 s)
1. Hook: Uske maathe par ek "D" ka nishaan tha. Isliye naam pada... Dotty.
2. Origin: Bandhavgarh ki sabse mashhoor baaghinon mein se ek. Lagbhag 2012 mein Magadhi mein paida hui. Maa, Sukhi Patiha. Behen, Spotty.
3. Magadhi years: Magadhi uska ghar bana. Wahin usne apne bachche paale. Kaha jaata hai, Khitauli ka aaj ka raja D1, usi ka beta hai.
4. The push: Phir wahi hua jo jungle mein hota hai. Uski apni betiyon ne use uske ilaake se bahar kar diya. Dotty Tala chali gayi, aur wahan phir maa bani. Do aur shaavak.
5. The end: 9 October. Manpur buffer, Majhkheta beat. Haathi gasht ke dauraan Dotty mrit mili. Umar lagbhag chaudah saal. Shuruaati jaanch mein maut prakritik batayi gayi.
6. The cubs: Peeche reh gaye do shaavak. Sirf pandrah se solah mahine ke. Ab forest department unpar nazar rakh raha hai.
7. Outro: Ek maa, jisne apni nasl se Bandhavgarh ko bhar diya. Aapne kabhi Dotty ko dekha tha? Comment mein batayiye.
