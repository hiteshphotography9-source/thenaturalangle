**Description:**
At 50°C the Thar has no shade. So where does a fox hide? Underground. This Short follows one typical night of the desert fox (the white-footed fox, Vulpes vulpes pusilla) from burrow to burrow: the tunnels, the big ears that hear prey underground, the all-round menu, the pups, and the threats to its home.

Fact vs estimate: litter size of about 3 to 5 pups is an approximate range and sources vary. The pounce behaviour shown is the red fox family's signature hunting move. No count of desert foxes in India exists. IUCN "Least Concern" is the status of the red fox worldwide, not an Indian population count. The story is a typical night based on field studies, not one animal's diary.

Where: India's dry west (Thar in Rajasthan, Great and Little Rann of Kachchh in Gujarat). Maps are illustrative.
