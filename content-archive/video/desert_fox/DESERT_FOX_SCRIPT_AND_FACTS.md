# Desert fox story Short / Reel: "A Night in the Thar" (92 s, 1080x1920)
Working title: **The Desert Fox's Night Shift**. A typical day told as a story, based on field studies. It is not one animal's biography, and the photos are different foxes (stated on the last frame).

## Hinglish VO script (about 235 words). Record one block per scene, 1 s pause between blocks.

**[1 HEAT, 0-9 s]**
Pachaas degree ke kareeb ki dopahar. Registan mein koi chhaya nahi. Phir ye lomdi kahan chhupti hai? Jawab aapke pairon ke neeche hai.

**[2 THE DEN, 9-21 s]**
Registani lomdi zameen ke neeche rehti hai. Uska bil ek bhool bhulaiya hai, kai raaste, kai darwaaze. Kabhi saahi ke purane bil mein, kabhi Rann ke namak ke dheron mein.

**[3 DUSK + WHERE, 21-33 s]**
Shaam hoti hai, garmi tootti hai, aur wo bahar aati hai. Ye sirf Rajasthan aur Gujarat ke sookhe ilaake ki jaanwar hai. Ise safed pairon wali lomdi bhi kehte hain.

**[4 EARS + POUNCE, 33-46 s]**
Raat ko uske bade kaan radar ban jaate hain. Zameen ke neeche chuhe ki halki khadkhad bhi wo pakad leti hai. Phir ek chhalaang, seedha neeche, aur shikaar uske munh mein. Lomdi parivaar ka ye khaas daav hai.

**[5 THE MENU, 46-58 s]**
Par ye sirf chuhon par nahi jeeti. Kide, phal, chhote jaanwar, chhipkali, kabhi pakshi. Jo mil jaye, wahi menu. Isi liye ye wahan tik paati hai jahan khaana mushkil hai.

**[6 PUPS, 58-71 s]**
Bil ke andar nanhe bachche paida hote hain, aam taur par teen se paanch. Teen chaar mahine tak maa baap unhe sambhalte hain, phir ek raat wo bhi bahar kadam rakhte hain.

**[7 THREATS, 71-83 s]**
Par registan badal raha hai. Vilayati babool khule ilaake ko ghera rahi hai. Awara kutte, sadkein, kheti aur shehar bhi. Aur is lomdi ki sahi ginti kisi ke paas nahi.

**[8 CLOSE, 83-92 s]**
Agli baar shaam ko registan mein ho, to peeche mudkar dekhiye. Kya aapne kabhi Registani lomdi dekhi hai? Kahan? Comment mein zaroor batayiye.
