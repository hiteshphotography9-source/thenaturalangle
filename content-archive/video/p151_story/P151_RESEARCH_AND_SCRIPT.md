# Tigress P-151 (Panna): research + script (draft 1)

## Confidence key
HIGH = two or more independent reports. MED = one solid report. LOW = single blog/forum, do not state as fact.

## Identity and lineage
- Daughter of T1, the first tigress brought to Panna (from Bandhavgarh, March 2009). HIGH (PTI via ThePrint, Feb 2023).
- T1: died of old age on Wed 1 Feb 2023, aged 17; 13 cubs in her life (Field Director Brijendra Jha). HIGH.
- Panna's ID pattern: first digit = mother (1 = T1), middle = litter number, last = cub number in litter (T141 = T1, 4th litter, 1st cub). A video description calls P-151 the first cub of T1's fifth litter. MED. T1's last litter was reportedly four cubs in 2016, so a 2016 birth is likely, but no source states her birth date. NOT FOUND.
- Father: NOT FOUND. A travel blog says male P-621 fathered the cubs she is "currently rearing" and later ceded the area to another male (undated). LOW.

## Territory
- Madla zone, Panna Tiger Reserve. Ken river banks; she marks territory on both banks and leaves sub-adult cubs near the bank when she does (photo essay). MED.
- Madla gate is on the Panna-Khajuraho road just past the Ken bridge. HIGH.

## Litters (reports conflict on exact counts and dates)
1. One cub. Field Director K S Bhadoria (Oct 2019): "T-151" seen with one cub. MED (label "T-151" may be a typo).
2. Two cubs, born about early 2021. Smithsonian contest photo: 8-month-old cub, Nov 2021, mother P-151 left the cub under a tree to find food. A forum post (Jan 2022) says her 2 cubs were female, nearly a year old. MED/LOW.
3. Four cubs. Seen 2 Feb 2023 heading to a waterbody, about 3 months old, her third litter (PTI). Panna Tiger Reserve X post: P151 with four cubs at Madla (Mar 2023). HIGH.
4. Two or three cubs. Sightings Apr 2025 (2 cubs drinking, 5-6 months old per guide), May 2025 (3 cubs crossing the road at Madla). A Dec 2024 blog calls this her fourth litter, 3 cubs. Reports conflict on 2 vs 3. MED.

## Fights and rivalry
- 30 June 2026: a roughly 17-month-old cub of P-151 died after a clash with a male tiger near Mandla gate (about 2 km inside, Diggi/Dikki Talaiya, Kanchan Nala area). Amar Ujala names the male P-661 and calls the cub female. ETV Bharat, Banda News and Navabharat report it as a territorial conflict; some say P-151 had two cubs at the time. No official statement found. MED. Say "reportedly".
- Sister rivalry with P-141: one forum post (Jan 2022) says the sisters from different litters scrapped, caught on camera by tourists. NOT confirmed by any news source I could open. LOW. Do not use as fact.
- Kills: no report found of P-151 killing a sambar. The creator's own trip caption says news came that P-151 killed a sambar. Use his first-hand account, not the web.

## Context
- Panna: zero tigers 2009, 59 by 2021, tigers dispersing, carrying capacity pressure (a news report notes 39 tigers against capacity for 30 at one point; undated).
- A 2026 feature (The Week, 25 Jul 2026) discusses dispersal and conflict as numbers grow.

## Gaps (not found)
Birth date, father, exact litter dates, cub sexes and fates for litters 3 and 4, official statement on the June 2026 death, any independent report of the sister fight.
