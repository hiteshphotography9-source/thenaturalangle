# Style reference: face-led long-form (from the two links you sent)

Videos analysed: Hj7GAdbODEo (17:52, Instagram growth, Hindi) and 1MLGhb-jvws (about 32 min, AI tools tutorial). Both are 16:9 talking-head videos with heavy motion graphics. Source: vidIQ video analysis.

## The style in one line
Face-led A-roll that never goes quiet: a cut or a graphic every 1.5 to 4 seconds, graphics float BEHIND the speaker (cutout mask), numbered chapters, whoosh and pop on every element, a low beat under the voice.

## What they do (and how we copy it for wildlife)
| Reference does | We do |
|---|---|
| Average shot length 1.5 to 4 s | Same. A cut, zoom or graphic at least every 3 s. |
| Floating 3D cards around the speaker, some BEHIND him (person cutout) | Photo cards (tiger, leopard, bird), map card, stat badge, floating around you, partly behind you. |
| Snap and punch zooms 110 to 120 percent on key phrases | Same, on the key word. Slow 3 percent push in between. |
| Whip zoom with directional blur after a title | Same, out of every chapter card. |
| Big numbered chapter cards (01, 02, #1) on a gradient | Same, lime numerals, chapter name below. |
| Node and mind-map diagrams with glowing lines | Fight/rescue maps, who-called-what, jeep and tiger diagrams. |
| Rounded pill badges, bento cards, dark mode | Same shapes in our lime and dark palette. |
| Whoosh on every card, pop on every icon, sub-bass on titles, lo-fi beat ducked under voice | Same. |
| PiP face in a rounded box over full-screen graphics (video 2) | Same for map and diagram sections. |
| Accent colour on key words (orange/blue, terracotta) | Lime on key words. |
| Open loop: promise the best tip at the end | Same: "the rule that decides everything comes last". |

## Rules we keep from our own channel
- English captions, no text over animal faces.
- Label estimates and field claims.
- Only your photos and your clips unless you say otherwise.

## What it needs that Long 1 did not have
Long 1 was photo-led with only 5 short talking-head clips. This style is FACE-led, so it needs the full footage of you speaking for the segments we edit.
