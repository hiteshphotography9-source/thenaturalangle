# Ranthambore reel: VO script (Hinglish, about 42 s)

Record in one calm, close-mic take. Leave about 0.4 s of silence between lines. Do not rush, I will tighten the pauses. Read the numbers as written.

| Time | Scene on screen | Say this |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 to 0:04 | Map of Rajasthan zooms into Sariska, big "0" | 2005 mein Sariska ki report aayi: ek bhi tiger nahi bacha. |
| 0:04 to 0:08 | Ranthambore tiger photo | Par sirf 150 kilometre door, Ranthambore ke tigers bilkul theek the. |

## Delivery notes
- Line 1 is the hook. Say it flat and serious, like a news line. Slow on "ek bhi tiger nahi bacha".
- Lines 3 and 4 have a small lift in energy. Line 5 is the warm part.
- Last line: smile while you say it. It is a question to your community, not an ad.
- Safe to change words to your natural Hinglish, but keep the facts: 2005, 150 km, 2008, ST-2, Rajmata, 30+, 2025, Mukundra, Ramgarh.

## Two alternate hooks (A/B test the first 3 seconds)
1. Ek poora tiger reserve, tigers se khaali ho gaya tha. Phir Ranthambore ne use bachaya.
2. Sariska mein ek bhi tiger nahi bacha tha. Ek tigress ne sab badal diya.
