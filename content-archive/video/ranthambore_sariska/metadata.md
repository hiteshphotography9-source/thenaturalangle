## YOUTUBE SHORT DESCRIPTION
In 2005 a report said Sariska had no tigers left. In 2008, tigers from Ranthambore were airlifted in, among them a tigress called ST-2. Today Sariska has 30+ tigers (reports vary) and India's first tiger memorial honours her.
