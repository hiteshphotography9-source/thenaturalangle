## Caption (Instagram, Facebook, YouTube Shorts description)
Delhi's AQI is 367 today. What about the birds?

Every winter, Delhi-NCR's wetlands (Okhla, Najafgarh Jheel, Sultanpur, Dhanauri) host migratory birds. This year the smog is back.

What I found:
- Fine particles can inflame bird lungs (research review, 2025).
- One east Delhi clinic treated about 25 birds with irritated eyes in a few weeks (Dec 2025). That is not a census.
- Najafgarh Jheel: 27,673 birds (2021) to 10,592 (2022), Asian Waterbird Census.
- Sultanpur: 9,026 (2023) to 2,686 (2024).
- Delhi's 22 km Yamuna stretch is among India's most polluted.

It is not only the air. Drying wetlands, dirty water, dust and building, and warmer winters matter too. Experts still debate how much is air pollution alone. Counts change with year, rain and method.
