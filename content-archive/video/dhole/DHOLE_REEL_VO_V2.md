# Dhole reel: Hinglish VO script v2 (about 65-70 s), comeback angle added

[HOOK] Jungle mein tiger aur leopard ke alawa ek aur predator hai, jise underestimate karna galti hai.

[WHO] Ye hai Dhole. Indian wild dog. Wazan bas 10 se 20 kilo. Lekin ye kabhi akela shikaar nahi karta.

[PACK] 5 se 12 ka pack, kabhi kabhi 30 se bhi zyada. Ghane jungle mein ye seeti jaisi awaaz se ek doosre ko signal dete hain.

[HUNT] Milkar apne se kai guna bade sambar aur chital ko gira dete hain. Aur tiger ko uske shikaar se bhaga dene ke records bhi hain.

[FALL] Lekin inki kahani dard bhari hai. Apne purane range ke 75 percent se zyada hisse se ye gayab ho chuke hain. Bharat mein inhe "vermin" maan kar 1972 tak maara gaya. Aaj poori duniya mein lagbhag 950 se 2,200 breeding adults hi bache hain.

[COMEBACK] Par ab kuch badal raha hai. Assam ke Kaziranga-Karbi Anglong mein, lagbhag 30 saal baad, camera trap mein ek dhole dikha. 2026 mein Manipur ke Shirui mein pehli baar record hua. Aur Madhya Pradesh ke Ratapani mein bhi pehli baar.

[BINOCULARS] Inhe dekhna aasaan nahi, ye pal bhar mein gayab ho jaate hain. Isliye safari pe main hamesha binoculars rakhta hoon. Link description mein hai.

[CTA] Comeback? Abhi nahi. Lekin har naya record ek wajah hai us jungle ko bachane ki. Aapne kabhi dhole dekha hai? Comment mein batao.
