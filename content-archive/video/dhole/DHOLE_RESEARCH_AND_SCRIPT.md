# Dhole Short: "The Predator People Forget Exists in Indian Forests"

Target length: 70-80 s. Format: 1080x1920 YT Short / IG Reel.

## Fact sheet (confidence labelled)

| Fact | Status | Source |
|---|---|---|
| IUCN status: Endangered | Verified | IUCN / Canid Specialist Group |
| Global: 4,500-10,500 dholes in total, of which 949-2,215 are mature (breeding-age) adults | Verified (Kamler et al. 2015 estimate; may be revised) | canids.org, threatenedtaxa.org |
| Gone from more than 75% of historic range (Asia-wide figure, NOT India-only) | Verified | canids.org, National Geographic |
| Pleistocene range covered much of Western and Central Europe; extinct there at the end of the last Ice Age | Verified | Wikipedia (European dhole), fossil literature |
| India: occupies about 249,606 sq km, roughly 49% of potential habitat (Srivathsa et al. 2020) | Verified | canids.org, WorldAtlas quoting Srivathsa |
| Strongholds: Western Ghats, Central India, North-East; Western Ghats is likely the largest population in the world | Verified (habitat-based inference, not a census) | canids.org, NCBS/WCS |
| There is NO national population count for India | Verified | canids.org |
| Wayanad WLS: about 50 dholes, 12-14 per 100 sq km (India's first population estimate) | Verified | NCBS / WCS India |
| Treated as vermin and bounty-hunted in India until the Wildlife Protection Act, 1972 | Verified | Down To Earth, Sanctuary Nature Foundation |
| Pack of 5-12, sometimes merging into 30+ | Verified | Discover Wildlife, IMSc |
| Whistle call to coordinate in dense forest ("whistling dog") | Verified | Discover Wildlife, Dublin Zoo |
| Kill prey many times their own weight (sambar, chital) | Verified | San Diego Zoo |
| Pups eat first at kills; adults regurgitate food for pups and the mother | Verified | San Diego Zoo |
| Antagonistic, sometimes fatal clashes with tigers and leopards are documented but rare; packs chasing tigers off kills is recorded | Verified (WCS India wording) | WCS India |
| "Dholes kill healthy adult tigers" | NOT verified. Old shikar stories (Kenneth Anderson) only. DO NOT claim | |
| Dhole first originated in India | NOT supported. The species spread across Eurasia; India is where it survives best. Script frames it that way | |

## Hinglish VO script (about 210 words, about 75 s)

**[HOOK 0-4 s]**
Jungle mein tiger aur leopard ke alawa ek aur predator hai, jise underestimate karna galti hai.

**[WHO 4-10 s]**
Ye hai Dhole. Indian wild dog. Size mein bas ek bade kutte jitna, 12 se 20 kilo. Lekin ye kabhi akela shikaar nahi karta.

**[PACK 10-20 s]**
Dhole pack mein rehte hain, 5 se 12 ke group mein, aur kabhi kabhi 30 se bhi zyada. Ghane jungle mein ye ek doosre ko seeti jaisi awaaz se signal dete hain. Isliye inhe "whistling dog" bhi kehte hain.

**[HUNT 20-30 s]**
Pack milkar apne se kai guna bade sambar aur chital ko thaka thaka ke girata hai. Aur shikaar ke baad sabse pehle khaate hain pack ke bachche.

**[TIGER 30-38 s]**
Aur ye tiger se bhi nahi darte. Records hain jahan dhole pack ne tiger ko uske shikaar se bhaga diya. Aur kuch rare cases mein ye ladaai jaanleva bhi rahi.

**[BINOCULARS 38-46 s]**
Inhe dekhna aasaan nahi. Ye ghane jungle mein pal bhar mein nikal jaate hain. Isliye safari pe main hamesha binoculars rakhta hoon. Door ki movement pehle pakad mein aa jaati hai. Link description mein hai.

**[HISTORY 46-58 s]**
Ek waqt tha jab dhole Europe se lekar Russia, China aur poore Asia mein phaile hue the. Aaj apne purane range ke 75 percent se zyada hisse se ye gayab ho chuke hain. Bharat mein to inhe "vermin" maan ke inaam rakha jaata tha. 1972 tak inhe maara jaata raha.

**[NOW 58-68 s]**
Aaj poori duniya mein sirf lagbhag 950 se 2,200 breeding adults bache hain. Aur inka sabse bada sahara hai India. Western Ghats, Central India aur North-East.

**[CTA 68-76 s]**
Agli baar safari pe sirf tiger mat dhoondhna. Ho sakta hai jungle ka sabse bada team player aapke saamne se nikal jaaye. Aapne kabhi dhole dekha hai? Comment mein batao.
