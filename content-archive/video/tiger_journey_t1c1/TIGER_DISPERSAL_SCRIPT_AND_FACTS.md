# Tiger T1-C1: "3,000 km walked, 315 km in a straight line" (75 s, 1080x1920)

## Correction to the idea
Your idea said "1000+ km, three states". Research says: **T1-C1 (Maharashtra, 2019) walked about 3,000 km cumulative, 315 km straight-line, over 225 days, across Maharashtra and Telangana (two states).** Press said 1,300 km at an earlier point. "Three states" belongs to the wider corridor, not to this tiger. The video uses the paper's numbers.
