# Great Hornbill story Short: "The Bird That Cannot Build Its Own Home" (87 s, 1080x1920)

Angle: not the sealed-nest story everyone knows. Story = a bird that can only inherit a home, so its whole life depends on giant old trees; it plants forest; it was hunted; hunters became guardians. Ends circular: "look at the tree".

## VO script (Hinglish), one block per scene, 1 s pause between blocks
**1. Hook (about 8 s)**
Ye parinda apna ghar bana nahi sakta. Use sirf viraasat mein milta hai. Great hornbill.
**2. The home (about 11 s)**
Na ye ped khodta hai, na ghonsla bunta hai. Use chahiye ek bada, purana ped, jis mein kudrat ne pehle se ek khokhal bana rakha ho. Anaimalai ki study mein, nest wale ped, aas paas ke pedon se saadhe attharah meter oonche, aur lagbhag ek meter zyada motay the.
**3. The tenant (about 11 s)**
Do se chaar kilo ka ye bhaari parinda. Jab udta hai, toh paron ki awaaz door se sunayi deti hai, jaise bhaap ka engine chal pada ho. Aur ye peela rang? Ye makeup hai. Tail ke paas ki gland ka tel, wo chonch aur paron par lagata hai.
**4. The helmet (about 8 s)**
Chonch ke upar ye khokhli topi, casque. Nar hornbill hawa mein ek doosre se casque takraate hain. Iska asli kaam, abhi tak poori tarah pata nahi.
**5. The map (about 11 s)**
Aise jungle ab kahan bache hain? Himalaya ki talhati, North-East ke pahadi jungle, aur Western Ghats ka ek alag hissa. Poore Asia mein ye Vulnerable list mein hai.
**6. The gardener (about 10 s)**
Par ye sirf jungle mein rehta nahi, jungle ugata bhi hai. Bade beej wale phal nigalta hai, aur door jaakar girata hai. Ek study mein 69 se 100 percent beej, maa ped se door mile. Shayad apne hi bhavishya ke ghar ugata hai.
**7. The price (about 8 s)**
Lekin iski casque ki keemat thi. Arunachal ke Nyishi samaj ke paramparik sir ke taj mein ise pehna jaata tha. Shikaar badha, aur hornbill ghatne laga.
**8. The turn (about 12 s)**
Phir ek anokha badlaav. Pakke mein kayi purane shikari, nest protector ban gaye. Hornbill Nest Adoption Program, dus gaon, dus rakhwale. 2012 se 2018 tak 119 chicks udaan bhar chuke. Aur taj mein asli chonch ki jagah, fibreglass ki chonch.
**9. Close (about 8 s)**
Agli baar jungle mein jao, toh sirf parinde ko mat dekhna. Ped ko dekhna. Wahi iska ghar hai. Follow The Natural Angle.
