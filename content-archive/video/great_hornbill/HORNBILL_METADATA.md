**Description**
The great hornbill cannot dig or build a nest. It needs a natural hole in a giant old tree. In the Anaimalai Hills, nest trees were on average 18.5 m taller and 0.85 m wider than other trees (24 nests studied).

In this Short: why the hornbill needs old forest, the hollow casque, where it lives in India (Himalayan foothills, North-East, Western Ghats), how it helps grow the forest by spreading large seeds, and how Nyishi nest protectors in Pakke, Arunachal Pradesh, are helping it recover. The Hornbill Nest Adoption Program reports 119 chicks fledged between 2012 and 2018.

IUCN status: Vulnerable. Maps are illustrative.
