Long1_Barahi_HD (1080p, about 800 MB) is split into 90 MB parts.
Download all Long1_Barahi_HD_*.part files into one folder, then join:

Windows (Command Prompt, inside that folder):
  copy /b Long1_Barahi_HD_*.part Long1_Barahi_HD_1080p.mp4

Mac / Linux:
  cat Long1_Barahi_HD_*.part > Long1_Barahi_HD_1080p.mp4

Do not rename the parts. Order is 00, 01, 02, ...
