**Description**
In 2009, Panna Tiger Reserve was declared to have no tigers. Today the recovery is one of India's biggest tiger stories, and the next question is where those tigers will live.

Full Panna story on my channel: [PASTE LONG VIDEO LINK]

What this Short covers: the 2009 zero, the translocation (two tigresses and a male, T3), the first cubs, 59 tigers by 2021, tigers dispersing out of the reserve, and the Ken-Betwa river link debate.

Notes: Photos are mine and illustrative. They are not the tigers named (T1, T2, T3). The map is schematic, not to scale. The 57 sq km figure is one study's estimate; other estimates differ.
